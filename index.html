Teaching prototype. Paleogeography follows the spirit of reconstructions by researchers such as Scotese, Torsvik & Cocks, and later plate models — drawn here as simple shapes so the idea is readable, not as publication-grade coastlines. Life and climate follow the ICS geologic time scale and standard paleobiology. Future slices are scenarios.

What this is — and is not

No person or model has ingested “all history and geography books of all nationalities.” That request is larger than a library, and older texts disagree with each other and with rocks.

This site is a structured teaching instrument. Content is written in American English from a late-20th- and 21st-century scientific consensus, with named uncertainties.

Use it to see change. Check primary papers before you cite a date.
